Kshetra Mahatyam & Heritage

🪷

Discover the rich spiritual legacy, sanctity and devotional traditions of Sri Kala Bhairava Devasthanam.

The Eternal Guardian: Lord Kala Bhairava

Lord Kala Bhairava is the fiercest manifestation of Lord Shiva, embodying Time (Kala) and fearless spiritual sovereignty. As the Kshetrapalaka, Lord Bhairava guards all sacred spaces and shields sincere devotees from negative energies, fear, and planetary adversities, especially during Rahukalam.

The Divine Mother: Sri Kalika Matha

Goddess Sri Kalika Matha represents the primordial cosmic energy (Adi Shakti) that destroys darkness and ignites divine consciousness. Devotees offer prayers to Ammavaru for good health, family protection, freedom from illusions, and prosperity.

Sri Kala Bhairava Swamy

Lord Kala Bhairava is known as the guardian of time and the ultimate protector against fear, debt, illnesses, and malefic astrological influences. Worshipping Lord Bhairava with black sesame deepam on Ashtami tithi grants spiritual liberation and worldly victory.

Moola Mantra: Om Hreem Bam Batukaya Apaduddharanaya Kuru Kuru Batukaya Hreem Om Namah Shivaya

Sri Kalika Matha

Goddess Sri Kalika Matha is the embodiment of compassion and invincibility. Devotees offer red hibiscus flowers and kumkumarchana to seek protection for children and families, fulfillment of noble desires, and destruction of ego.

Moola Mantra: Om Kreem Kalikaye Namah • Om Aim Hreem Kleem Chamundayai Vicche
